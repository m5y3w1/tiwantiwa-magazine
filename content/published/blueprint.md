# The Zero-Cost Publisher Blueprint

By utilizing static site generation and GitHub-backed content workflows, independent publishers achieve unlimited scaling potential without monthly SaaS tax.

## Architecture Highlights
- **Storage**: Markdown files in Git.
- **Delivery**: Cloudflare Pages or Vercel Edge.
- **Monetization**: Native modular ad slots and affiliate matrices.
