Agriculture remains the largest employer of labor in Nigeria, yet smallholder farmers have historically faced immense structural hurdles: unpredictable weather patterns, lack of credit access, and post-harvest losses due to poor logistics.

### Digitizing the Agricultural Value Chain

A new wave of agritech startups is deploying precision agriculture tools to change the equation. IoT-enabled soil sensors provide real-time data on moisture and nutrient levels; drone mapping helps optimize fertilizer application; and mobile marketplaces connect rural farmers directly with urban buyers, cutting out exploitative middlemen.

### Sustainable Economic Impact

By de-risking agricultural financing through verified data and insurance tech, investors are unlocking institutional capital for rural communities. Smart farming is proving that technology can transform traditional agriculture into a high-growth, sustainable economic engine.
